## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** Our own scenario: a guided, explainable instalment-plan journey in the German portal pilot, covering single-claim and multi-claim consumers with variable income. It includes a context-rich handoff to agents when the case needs a human.
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing for 12 months, the company will keep losing more than half of the started instalment journeys: 54% end without an agreement, and 31% of starters call an agent within 7 days. Agents will keep spending over 50% of their time on payment, legal and enforcement work. About 1 in 5 new plans will still break within 60 days, which erodes the value of purchased portfolios. Meanwhile, Riverty is rolling out consistent digital self-service across Europe, so it will fall behind its own rollout and its clients' expectations for compliant, efficient, transparent servicing. That puts mandates and portfolio-bid competitiveness at risk. (Assumption: the brief has no competitor data, so the "market position" part is an inference to validate.)
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** The user is currently forced to… guess. Mara opens the portal on her phone between shifts, can't tell whether a plan would be affordable, doesn't know why we ask about her income, and fears that a wrong answer or a missed payment will make things worse. Her options are to abandon the journey, dig for income documents she may not have, or call an agent and repeat everything. Outside our product, she's likely Googling "Ratenzahlung Inkasso", doing the maths in her banking app or a spreadsheet, or asking friends. (Hypothesis: this is based on the brief's research themes and still needs validating with real call reasons.)
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must solve the rising cost and weak repayment quality of instalment recovery (54% of journeys not completed, 31% agent contact, 19% of plans failing within 60 days) by addressing consumers' inability to tell what a safe, affordable plan looks like, why we ask what we ask, and what happens if they miss a payment.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For consumers like Mara (shift workers with variable income and one or more open claims), we will offer a guided portal journey that explains each question, proposes a plan she can realistically keep, and hands off to an agent with full context when needed, because we're losing most starters today, and our north-star metric (SSR) counts a plan as "resolved" the moment it is created, so we could hit our numbers while 1 in 5 plans fail.
